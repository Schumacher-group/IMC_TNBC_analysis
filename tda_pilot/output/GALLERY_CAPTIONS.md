# Day-1 point-cloud galleries — captions & provenance

Focal pair: **Tumour** (`Cancer cell` + `B7H4 Cancer cell`) vs **CD8** (`CD8 T cell` +
`Memory CD8 T cell`), definition `CD8_primary`, revision cohort (808 ROIs / 63 patients).
Feature: `im_dim1_total_persistence` = summed (death − birth) over all degree-1 features of
the **image** persistence diagram from the chromatic Delaunay (delaunay_cech) six-pack.
Coordinates are cell centroids in µm (Hyperion 1 px = 1 µm); tumour = red, CD8 = blue;
y-axis inverted (image coordinates). Generated by `day1_pointcloud_gallery.py` and
`day1_residual_gallery.py`. Descriptive only — no model fit.

---

## Fig A — `hist_im_persistence.png`
**Distribution of image degree-1 total persistence by response (808 ROIs).** Left: raw counts
(dashed lines = group medians, Non-Responder 1843, Responder 1403); right: group-normalised
density. Both groups overlap heavily; responders are shifted to lower persistence and
non-responders carry a heavier high-persistence tail (Cliff's δ = −0.168, R vs NR). Interpretation:
responders tend toward lower image persistence (more interspersed tumour/CD8), but the effect is
small with large overlap.

## Fig B — `gallery_high_persistence.png` and `gallery_low_persistence.png` (RAW sort)
**Point clouds of the 8 highest- and 8 lowest-persistence ROIs, sorted on raw image persistence.**
These are shown for completeness but are **size-confounded**: the highest-persistence ROIs are all
large, dense tumour fields (4,500–6,700 tumour cells) and the lowest are tiny, sparse ROIs
(26–218 tumour cells). The raw sort therefore contrasts cell count, not spatial arrangement, and
should not be read as a segregation comparison. (Motivates the size-matched galleries below.)

## Fig C — `gallery_resid_high.png` and `gallery_resid_low.png` (SIZE-MATCHED) — key figure
**Point clouds sorted by the size-adjusted residual of image persistence, within a mid-size band.**
Method: residual of `im_dim1_total_persistence` after OLS on (`n_tumour`, `n_other`); ROIs restricted
to the cohort interquartile band of total tumour+CD8 count (1,172–3,313 cells, n = 404) so the two
panels have comparable cell numbers. The contrast is therefore **spatial arrangement, not density**.

- **`gallery_resid_high.png` — HIGH residual = SEGREGATED.** Large coherent tumour masses with CD8
  confined to separate territories / the periphery; sharp red–blue boundaries; few mixed regions.
  This is the excluded-rim topology that produces large, persistent image-diagram loops.
- **`gallery_resid_low.png` — LOW residual = INTERSPERSED.** Fine-grained salt-and-pepper
  intermixing of tumour and CD8 throughout, with no large monochromatic regions. This is the
  infiltrated topology, in which the joint complex is "filled in" and image persistence is low.

**Takeaway:** size-adjusted image degree-1 total persistence is a validated, interpretable readout
of CD8–tumour **interspersion vs segregation** — the infiltration-vs-exclusion distinction that
squidpy neighbourhood enrichment conflates. Responders trend toward the interspersed (low-persistence)
end, CD8-specifically (control pairs flat) and robustly to NK/CD8 inclusion, though the response
association itself is small (δ ≈ −0.14 to −0.17 after size adjustment). Response mix at the residual
extremes is muddy (HIGH: 5 R / 3 NR; LOW: 6 R / 2 NR), consistent with a small effect — the galleries
validate the *feature*, not a response classifier.

## Fig D — `gallery_resid_high_CD8_NK_only.png` / `gallery_resid_low_CD8_NK_only.png`
**Size-matched galleries for the NK/CD8-only definition** (`CD8_NK_only`: tumour vs `NK/CD8`,
the activated GranzymeB+ cluster the manuscript describes as "NK/CD8 cells infiltrating cancer cell
groups"). Same method as Fig C (residual of image persistence after OLS on cell counts; mid-size band
492–2117 cells, n = 403). High residual panels show tumour masses with NK/CD8 in separate territories
(segregated); low residual panels show finer tumour/NK-CD8 intermixing (interspersed). The gradient is
**consistent in direction with the CD8_primary aggregate but visually weaker/noisier**, because NK/CD8
is a sparse population (~113 cells/ROI, ~7× fewer than CD8_primary). Quantitatively the NK/CD8-only
response signal is comparable after size adjustment (Cliff δ = −0.132 vs −0.138 for CD8_primary) but
much weaker on raw values (−0.026), i.e. NK/CD8-only is a thinner, noisier readout of the same topology,
not a stronger one. CD8_primary remains the cleanest focal definition.
